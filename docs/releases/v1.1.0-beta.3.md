# podssh v1.1.0-beta.3

The third beta of milestone M10, and the first one after the study of osewa
(`docs/research/osewa.md`): what a user feels on a slow or busy link is now
measured, and podssh's SSH client keeps a key's echo near the link's round
trip while a download runs on the same connection, against any sshd and
through each road; a session resumes in about a second after the client
moves; and a cold connect takes two round trips fewer. The relay role
gains the hardening that a tester asked for (GitHub #126 to #148). A beta is
a prerelease. Its tag never moves: a defect found in it goes into the next
beta.

## What to test again

What a user feels (the study of osewa, Q99):

- `podssh ping DEST --under-load` types 40 keys beside a download on the
  same connection and gives the echo's p50 and p95; `--connect-runs N`
  times N cold connects (T-660). `scripts/feel-matrix.py` runs each road
  through the four links of osewa's lab on the loopback.
- Typing-aware SSH windows: while a key waits for its echo, a bulk
  channel's window is held to the path's round trip and 40 ms, and each
  socket that podssh dials keeps 16 KiB unsent; where nobody types, a
  window grows to the path, up to 16 MiB on `--direct` (T-661, T-662). The
  server's messages are of 8 KiB, and a window is topped up once a quarter
  of it is used (T-670). Measured on the loopback, a key's echo beside a
  download: about 100 ms on a cellular link where OpenSSH's is 1.6 s, and
  0.4 s on a link of 1 Mbit/s where OpenSSH's is 16 s. The download while
  typing on a long fast link is slower than before (T-670, partial).
- Liveness in round trips: a spare link after three round trips of
  silence, 1 s to 3 s, and a check when the host wakes; a session resumed
  in 1.0 s after the client's new address, where it took 3 s (T-663).
- A faster cold connect: the key that a host took is signed in the first
  request, the pty's request and the command's go together, and a relay
  host's TLS group is kept (T-664): two round trips fewer, faster than
  OpenSSH's own client to the same sshd on the loopback's links.
- The resumable layer ends one direction (`END`), so a request, its end and
  the reply after it cross the relay role's layer road (T-632).

The relay role, `podssh relay serve` (GitHub #126 to #148):

- A name that the role does not dial gets one `403` that names no address
  (T-628, #126).
- It keeps no capability after its bind (T-629, #127); a list of private
  ranges that it may dial, `--routes` (T-635, #133); a share of the pairs
  for each key that may mint (T-630, #128); a turn for each session on a
  node's socket (T-648, #146); a target's bytes after the client's Close,
  as the contract says (T-671); a pair that halts without ending,
  `podssh relay halt` and `relay resume` (T-647, #145).
- `podssh pipe ws-listen:` answers a request that is not an upgrade with a
  page from `--cover DIR`, and an upgrade with no key with `--raw-upgrade`
  (T-639, T-645, #137, #143).
- A retired `PODSSH_*` variable is refused by name (T-634, #132); a node's
  lines keep their priority in the journal (T-640, #138).

## Check a download

```sh
T=v1.1.0-beta.3; B=podssh-x86_64-unknown-linux-musl   # or another file of the table
curl -fsSLO "https://github.com/Azathothas/podssh/releases/download/$T/$B"
curl -fsSLO "https://github.com/Azathothas/podssh/releases/download/$T/SHA256SUMS"
grep " $B\$" SHA256SUMS | sha256sum -c - && chmod +x "$B"
./"$B" --version   # podssh 1.1.0-beta.2 (the commit of the tag)
```

`podssh update --version v1.1.0-beta.3` from `v1.1.0-beta.2`, `v1.1.0-beta.1` or `v1.0.1`
takes it, checked by its signature; a build of a release follows releases
alone, so without `--version` it stays on `v1.0.1`. The provenance and the
signature are checked as for `v1.0.0` ([its notes](https://github.com/Azathothas/podssh/blob/main/docs/releases/v1.0.0.md)),
with `T=v1.1.0-beta.3`.

## How it was checked

By the operator's ruling Q88 (2026-11-03), until podssh is stable: the gate
green at the tag's commit, the release workflow by hand on it, and the short
form of its binary (`scripts/release-short.sh BINARY --net`); after the tag,
`release-proofs.yml` and `update-live.yml`. The whole release check and the
live runs of each road wait until then.

## What a beta does not have yet

- The whole check of a release from end to end with no human
  (`scripts/release-check.sh`, T-251): it waits (Q88).
- Signatures of the platforms: the Mac binaries are not signed with an
  Apple Developer ID, and the Windows binaries carry no Authenticode
  signature, so SmartScreen may warn. The proofs above stand in for them.

## Report a defect

Open an issue at <https://github.com/Azathothas/podssh/issues> with the
output of `podssh --version` and `podssh doctor --json`. Read that output
before you post it: it names this host's proxy and addresses, and never a
token or a password.
